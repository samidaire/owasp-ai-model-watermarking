  Thief, counterfeit key : BER = 0.000   extracted message = 'MALLORY!'
  -> Both parties 'prove' ownership of the same model.

  Can a judge who SEES the key and weights tell the counterfeit apart?
    key                         entry mean entry std    KS D  min margin  median margin
    owner (real)                   +0.0041    1.0029  0.0000        1.42           1.82
    forged, naive                  -0.0150    1.0050  0.0104        0.02           0.67
    forged, large-margin rows      -0.0120    0.9980  0.0099        1.43           1.71
    (KS 5% critical value 0.0142; margins in units of a random row's std)
