3

Our fix: lock the seed first · Demo D

Click
→ or sidebar D · Committed seed.
Show
Naive fix: 1,000/1,000 fakes pass. Real fix: 0/100,000 pass, best thief still 16/64 wrong. On the chart every try is far from the green “accept” line.
Say
“The owner publishes a hash of a secret seed at registration; the key and the mark both come from it. Now the thief must guess the seed. 100,000 tries, none came close. An even chance needs about 2.5 billion tries, around 196 hours on one CPU core. At 256 bits it is out of reach.”
